**Current statuses:** `water`, `clear-water`, `dirty-water`, `water-rising`, `boat-sinking`, and `looking-at-a-river` are KEEP; several neighbors are unclassified. No water page is currently Merge Review or Noindex Review.

- `water` vs `calm-water` / `clear-water` / `dirty-water` / `water-rising`: **distinct intent but weak differentiation risk**. The variants have different physical properties and reader questions; the broad page should teach how movement, visibility, and boundary-crossing change interpretation rather than repeat “water equals emotion.” Keep separate and repair/link where the distinction is not obvious.
- `ocean` vs `being-in-the-ocean`: **distinct intent but weak differentiation**. The former is the scale/environment question; the latter is immersion, exposure, and agency. Keep separate; add explicit hub and page-to-page context.
- `dirty-water` vs `falling-or-swimming-in-dirty-water`: **appropriately separate**, with an architecture/linking problem. One centers visibility/contact; the other adds falling, movement, and recovery. Do not merge.
- `water` vs `walking-on-water` and `clear-water` vs `water`: **architecture/linking problem**, not duplication. The action or visibility should determine the next path.
- `boat-sinking` vs `water` / `drowning`: **appropriately separate**. The failed boundary or coping vessel is the anchor; drowning centers loss of breathable agency.

**Recommended action:** First repair batch should pair `water`, `ocean`/`being-in-the-ocean`, and one action page. Preserve the existing water hub. No merge/noindex candidate.
