## Method

Each page was reviewed through its final composed content: opening, supporting description, interpretation lenses, context, scenarios, questions, and related-link reasons. Decisions used the reader-recognition, unique-insight, anchor, detail-reasoning, neighbor-replacement, human-voice, trust, and 2 a.m. usefulness tests.

The audit score was treated as a prompt, not a verdict. Missing questions alone did not establish independent value, and a strong score did not protect a page that duplicated another reader experience.

## Reclassification findings

### Pages removed from Improve

The eight Keep pages already demonstrate a specific anchor, meaningful conditional variables, a defensible neighbor distinction, and a useful reader response. Rewriting them would risk erasing human phrasing for little gain. Minor future maintenance is allowed, but none needs a repair batch.

### Merge Review

`being-chased-by-a-lion` and `lion-chasing-you` fail the replacement test. Their word order differs, but the reader experience, authority/power interpretation, chase logic, and outcomes are materially the same. No merge was executed. A later merge brief should select the stronger URL using search and link evidence, preserve the best opening, contextual caveats, five questions, home/dark/open-space scenarios, and real-animal/cultural context.

### Noindex Review

No new Noindex Review decision was made. Baby Smiling and Red Sky are the closest low-value risks, but each still has a plausible independent reader question that can be tested through one focused repair before indexing is reconsidered. This is not permission to pad either page.
