## Human approval evidence

### Being Lost

**Recognition:** “Where you were trying to go—and whether you felt panicked, curious, ashamed, or eventually relieved—helps distinguish a crisis of direction from a temporary period of adjustment.”

**Unique insight:** “Being lost is not always a sign that you lack direction; when the destination is clear but no route feels workable, the dream may concern competing guidance rather than an absence of purpose.”

**Independent value:** Lost in a Building remains about enclosed rooms, institutions, and the structure of a particular problem. Being Lost now examines destination, route, available guidance, social separation, and outcome across settings.

### Being Stuck in an Elevator

**Recognition:** “Notice the intended floor, direction of travel, who entered with you, and whether the elevator stalled, dropped, shook, or merely refused to open.”

**Unique insight:** “An elevator represents movement delegated to a mechanism. Unlike stairs, it can carry you quickly, but you cannot create that movement through effort alone.”

**Independent value:** Running but Can’t Move Fast concerns personal effort producing too little movement. The elevator page concerns progress controlled by a system, gatekeeper, timetable, or another person.

### Unable to Call or Communicate

**Recognition:** “Being unable to call or communicate in a dream often points to a message, warning, or need for connection that cannot reach its destination.”

**Unique insight:** “Being unable to communicate is not one failure with one meaning. The exact point where connection breaks provides the clearest clue.”

**Independent value:** Phone Not Working remains focused on technology, malfunction, and access. This page follows the human communication chain through access, expression, delivery, listening, trust, and ambivalence.
