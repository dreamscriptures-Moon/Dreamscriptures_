## Category findings

- **Highest improvement burden:** Water, transformation/life transitions, relationships, and spiritual/biblical pages. Their recurring issue is not missing fields; it is broad symbolism that needs a visible variable or response chain.
- **Most common merge pattern:** singular/plural or near-identical biblical/relationship intent (`shooting-stars`/`shooting-star`, `marrying-a-stranger`/`getting-married-to-a-stranger`, `noahs-ark`/`ark`).
- **Most common noindex reason:** a vivid but narrow scene whose current interpretation does not yet establish an independent question beyond a broader neighbor.
- **Unexpectedly strong pages:** Driving, Eyes, Saying No, Ex Texting You, Cheating on Your Partner, Water, Seeing Jesus, Crows, 11:11, Hair Falling Out, Cemetery, Harvesting, and Water Rising all passed as KEEP despite the structural flag.

## Heuristic review

The content audit is over-flagging pages that already have five core sections, contextual scenarios, and explained related links but fall below its rich-section word threshold. It is under-sensitive to conceptual repetition and inherited generic tails. The triage decision therefore uses the audit as a diagnostic signal, not as the editorial verdict.
