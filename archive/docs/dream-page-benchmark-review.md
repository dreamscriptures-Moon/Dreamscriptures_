# Dream-page benchmark review — Phase 1

This is the first calibration pass using the rubric in `docs/dream-page-editorial-standard.md`. Scores are editorial review aids, not automated SEO grades. A missing spiritual or biblical section is not counted as a weakness when it would not add honest value.

Every future benchmark review must also record the **2 a.m. reader test**: the exact detail that makes the reader feel understood and the useful insight they receive beyond a generic symbol definition. A page cannot become a benchmark through scoring alone.

## Standard demonstrated by the benchmark set

The benchmark pages establish that excellence does not mean every page has identical sections. It means the page makes the most important distinctions for its subject:

- **Common symbols** need behavior, setting, and emotion.
- **Relationship dreams** need memory, attachment, present triggers, boundaries, and non-literal possibilities.
- **Body dreams** need real sensations and health-related uncertainty without diagnosis.
- **Spiritual dreams** need discernment, source context, and alternatives such as stress or sleep phenomena.
- **Threat dreams** need safety-aware language without turning fear into evidence.
- **Ordinary dreams** need practical usefulness without being made artificially profound.
