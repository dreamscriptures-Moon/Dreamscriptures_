### Page: Ark

**Primary intent:** Understand an ark as refuge, preservation, preparation, or a container carrying what matters through danger; the reader may or may not mean Noah's Ark.  
**Core dream experience:** Seeing, entering, building, or travelling in an ark, with Noah, animals, flood, or covenant imagery sometimes absent.  
**Current anchor:** Preservation through a threatening transition.  
**Closest neighbor:** `noahs-ark`.  
**Replacement-test result:** **Essentially yes in the current content.** Both pages give nearly the same protection, refuge, preparation, faith, survival, and new-beginning answer.  
**Title-removal test:** Most of either interpretation transfers with minor noun changes. Noah, animals, flood, obedience, covenant, and rainbow are distinct details, but can survive as a section within a broad Ark resource.  
**Independent value:** Broad *ark* intent is real; two current resources are not. Ark is the stronger destination because it can branch into Noah's Ark, the Ark of the Covenant, and non-specific refuge imagery.  
**Decision:** **MERGE REVIEW** — likely `noahs-ark` into `ark`.  
**Reasoning:** The pair currently duplicates the reader journey. A combined page must distinguish **biblical tradition → possible symbolic interpretation → personal context** and must not present protection as a promise, predict disaster, or imply divine instruction.  
**Recommended next action:** Create a separate merge brief preserving Noah-specific scenarios and the Genesis source. Do not merge in this phase.

## Pair analysis

### Room with Many People / Small Crowded Room

**Why the pages are different:** The first asks, “What is my social position among these people?” The second asks, “What happens when this space no longer gives me enough room or a usable exit?” Recognition, belonging, and anonymity drive the first; density, bodily compression, sensory load, and constrained movement drive the second.

The broad page should link to Small Crowded Room when lack of space or exit dominated. The small-room page should link back when identity of the people and social role mattered more than compression.

### Intimacy with an Animal / Strange Creature

**Why the pages are different:** The known-animal page can reason from species, learned associations, familiarity, and prior relationship. The strange-creature page lacks that stable identity, so ambiguity, otherness, and agency become the interpretation. Neither page should treat closeness as proof of literal sexual desire.
